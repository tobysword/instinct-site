Changelog

What's new.

Release notes for every version are also on GitHub releases.

1.0.0
Stable · release date TBA

The overlay is rebuilt around short, honest reads instead of tips and sliders.

Added

  • Reads card: after a goal, two short facts from this match against the lobby, your teammate or your usual. Never advice.
  • Left glance stats + minimap: up to 6 stat tiles per mode (pick from 18) with a lobby strip showing a rank pill per player, and our own minimap.
  • Rank estimates per player, per playlist. ~ marks a low-confidence estimate from scoreboard events only. Your own Playing like shows confidence (x/4), trend and the reasons up and down.
  • Post-match "Reads this game" and "vs your usual".
  • Toxic mode PRO: off by default, on your overlay only, never sent to the game. Swearing is a separate 18+ toggle.
  • Hide player names for streamers.
  • Opt-in crash reports (off by default).

Changed

  • Renamed to Instinct. Settings and history stay in %APPDATA%\rl-companion.
  • Downloads are now Instinct-Setup.exe and Instinct-portable.zip. The old RL Coach Companion program is removed on install and your data is kept.
  • One stable update channel.
  • Local match history and sign-in are free. Session tracking and growth trends are part of Instinct Pro.

Removed

  • Text tips, playstyle sliders and the rotation arrow. The live data can't support them honestly: it has no passes, possession, boost pads or positions, and only events for opponents.
