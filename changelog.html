What's new

Recent releases.

The app updates itself. Full notes for every version are on GitHub releases.

3.7.0

20 November 2026
  • Learn how to play every mode. The new Learn tab (Instinct Pro) is a library picked by playlist, with Rotations, Mechanics, Videos, and a Mode guide for the extra modes. It starts with the Rumble item guide, and new content arrives without an update.
  • Get credit for your double taps. Double taps in saved replays are detected far more often, with fewer false reports.

3.6.0

20 November 2026
  • See every fundamental measured. All fifteen Film Room rows now show your stat against your usual, including clears, jumps in net, 50/50s, boost, and what you do after a demo.
  • Keep one thing in mind every game. Make a fundamental your Focus and its cue shows under Reads on the overlay until you are back to your usual.
  • Land the mechanics you use. The Film Room now covers every mechanic you try, open-play speed flips included, with 3D models of the right way.

3.5.0

19 November 2026
  • Watch your mistakes and how to fix them. The new Film Room lists fifteen fundamentals with your stat against your usual. Open one to play your costliest moment from a saved replay next to the right way, step by step.
  • See your game in 3D. Moments play on Rocket League's arena to its real shape, with every car moving and turning as it did in your replay.
  • Know which mechanics to work on. Mechanics for your rank shows how often you land the ones worth using up to your target rank, with 3D models of the right way.

3.4.0

18 November 2026
  • Pick your playlist right on the page. The playlist menu moved from the top bar into Progress and Team, at the top of what it changes.
  • See your last 10 games or all of them. Switch Progress and Team between Last 10 games and All games in one click.
  • Look at one playlist in a session. Open a session and pick one of its playlists to see just those games, with the others faded.

3.3.0

17 November 2026
  • See every topic at a glance. Where you played opens on the field from above, with six topics beside it, each showing the answer furthest from your usual. Click one and the camera flies in.
  • Show only the moments you want. Each question has its own chips, like your team or theirs next, air or ground, or each part of the goal. Click one to show only those.
  • Watch your shots and saves fly. Shots and saves from a saved replay are drawn as arcs: dotted when slow, long dashes when fast, with where a miss went and where the ball went after a save.
  • Find questions by clearer names. Ball Possession After Touch, Shot Placement, Defensive Recoveries, and more.

3.2.2

17 November 2026
  • Choose your Coach shape stats. Default, Auto, and My stats now save and show in game.
  • Read replays more accurately. Every touch sits at the moment it happened, so shots, saves, goals, and 50/50s from a replay are closer to the game. Replays from the last 30 days are read again.
  • Fewer stats, clearer shape. Slow is gone from the shape and every stat list.

3.2.1

16 November 2026
  • Pick on the field. A strip of small cards under the field, one per zone, part of the goal, or moment. Click one, or double-click the field to pick what is there.
  • Smoother replays. Pause holds the frame, Play carries on from there, and Play all stops at the end.

3.2.0

16 November 2026
  • The 3D field answers first. Each tab asks the same four questions and writes the answer out, marked against your usual. Click one and the field turns to show the moments behind it.
  • Side by Side. These games on one field, your usual on another. Drag either one and both turn.
  • Replays for a single game. Save the replay and the match lists its key moments on every tab, then plays the seconds around each one with a sentence on what happened.
  • Replays read in more detail. Every touch knows where the ball went next, and replays from the last 30 days are read again on their own.

3.1.3

15 November 2026
  • A lighter overlay. It does about a fifth of the work per update in game.
  • More accurate numbers. The shot card no longer counts aerial goals twice, Trends follow the playlist you pick, and "How you played" for a group compares each playlist with its own ranks.
  • Fixes. Creating an account in first-time setup works again, the Team page no longer gets stuck after joining or leaving, and deleting a match also removes its cloud copy.
  • Clearer wording. One name per stat, the same rank short names everywhere, and no mentions of removed features.

3.1.2

15 November 2026
  • A shot map on the overlay. Every shot this game on their half, colored by goal, saved, post or no goal, with a line to the goal. It also counts shots Rocket League misses: post hits and shots blocked on the way in.
  • A save-replay reminder you can't miss. When a match ends, a popup in the middle of the screen until the replay is saved.
  • Joining a team is free, and so are share cards. Your games are shared with your team automatically, and the team record counts everyone.
  • Left and right fixed. They were swapped on the shot card and the 3D field.

3.1.1

15 November 2026
  • The shot card has its own spot. It sits below Reads on the overlay and stays up all game with your last shot: power, height, what you hit it from and the angle. Switch it off in Settings.

3.1.0

14 November 2026
  • Every touch, explained. The 3D field shows each touch as a dot colored by what it led to: a pass, a clear, a shot, a 50/50, or a lost ball shaded by how costly it was.
  • Positioning, rebuilt. Your role at every moment (first man, second man, last back) and your positioning mistakes on the field: booms over your head, double commits, caught up-field.
  • A 50/50s tab. Won, neither or lost, by situation, surface and the part of your car that met the ball.
  • Sharper Shooting and Saves. Where your shots crossed the goal, the kind of shot, power and angle; every shot on your goal from where it was taken to where it was stopped.
  • Shot card. A quick read on the overlay after each of your shots.
  • Why this rating. The stats that moved your Playing like are marked on your shape.
  • Playlist groups, a reworked Team page for up to 20 players, and a fix for Progress freezing or drifting.

3.0.0

14 November 2026
  • One view of how you played. Progress, a single match and a session now show the same thing: Playing like, your shape against your target rank, stat by stat, and the 3D field.
  • Shooting, Saves and Recoveries on the 3D field. New tabs beside Touches and Positioning, from the replays you save. Each has its own panel that filters the field.
  • Stat by stat, shorter. The three stats furthest from your target rank lead; open the rest when you want them.
  • Settings by subject. Seven sections, one home for each setting.
  • Target rank is easier to find, and When you play is on Progress too.
  • The separate "From your replay" cards are gone; what they showed for shots and recoveries is on the field.

2.9.0

14 November 2026
  • Where you play, in 3D. A field you can turn: your positioning as a glowing cloud (from saved replays), every touch, and the touches that scored.
  • Filter it. Click a third of the field or a height to see only that part.
  • On Progress, in a match breakdown and in each session.

2.8.0

13 November 2026
  • Your data follows your account. Matches, sessions, settings and overlay layout are kept per account on your PC. A different account signing in starts with its own.
  • Overlay panels always show. The Coach and Session panels no longer collapse, and they can sit higher on screen.
  • On the coach shape, a stat below your target rank's range is now red, like one past SSL.
  • Landings, ground time and touches are read on the right scale, so Landings no longer shows far past SSL.
  • Start and end a session from the top of the Sessions page.

2.7.0

13 November 2026
  • Playing like reads more of your game. It now also reads your touches (how high and how hard), your time on the ground and in the air, and your boost in the air. Learned from 6,300 ranked replays, a single game lands closer to the real rank in every playlist.
  • Rank bands on the coach shape. The ring is your target rank's normal range for each stat. A red edge marks where SSLs stop, and Reads calls it a Redline when you go past it.
  • The shape leads with the stats that tell the ranks apart best. With Instinct Pro, pick your own stats and their order, or let Auto choose for your rank.
  • Saves are read against the shots the other team took, so a quiet game no longer reads as a weak one.
  • Tabs across the top, a short first-time setup, and deleting a match or session. An account is now required.
  • The phone companion is gone: the app no longer opens a network port.

2.6.0

11 November 2026
  • Playing like, rebuilt. It now reads only the stats that rise steadily from Bronze to SSL, each pushing your reading only its own way. A GC1 game reading SSL is now rare, and single games land closer to your real rank.
  • The coach shape. In game, your stats against your target rank's average as one shape: inside the line is below, outside is above. The gap bars are gone.
  • Reads after each goal: one stat to keep and one to shift, with a short cue and what changed since the last goal.
  • The post-game card shows the whole game's shape with your usual, and your level over your last ten games sits beside every reading.
  • Forfeits and late joins in casual are now rated and saved, tagged so they don't skew your level.

2.5.0

6 November 2026
  • Replay analysis, now free and much deeper. Save a replay and that match shows your rotation mistakes, what each touch led to, where your shots crossed the goal line, how cleanly you landed and recovered, which mechanics came off, and the moment behind each goal against.
  • Progress tracks these over your replayed games; Sessions lists the games you haven't saved a replay for yet.
  • Heatmaps, rotation, kickoffs and the Deep dive are no longer Instinct Pro: all replay analysis is free.
  • In game, a Session panel (Instinct Pro) shows your record, streak and this session's games.

2.4.1

5 November 2026
  • The post-game card draws its stats like the app: a bar from your target rank's average, with a short legend that no longer gets cut off.
  • Stats say Better or Worse (what higher or lower ranks do) instead of above or below.
  • A match started straight after free play now gets its post-game card and counts in your session.
  • A cleaner Match page: the target rank picker sits in the last game's card.

2.4.0

5 November 2026
  • Instinct Pro subscriptions: $4.99 a month or $35.88 a year, with a 7-day free trial. Pro turns on by itself after checkout.
  • Sessions show how you played with the same stat bars as Match, biggest changes first.
  • Positioning from a saved replay is one clear visual on Match, Progress and Sessions.
  • Sessions only call a playlist above or below your usual after 3 rated games and a gap bigger than the normal game-to-game swing.

2.3.0

5 November 2026
  • A new Playing like engine for Duel, Doubles and Standard, trained on about 39,000 real ranked games. Ten games now average within one rank of a player's real rank 84 to 87 % of the time.
  • Saved replays sharpen that match: your positioning and a sharper read, read automatically on your PC.
  • A redesigned app: Match, Progress, Sessions and Team each lead with one clear answer.
  • Every stat explains itself, and the post-game card stays for the whole post-game lobby.

2.2.0

4 November 2026
  • Rank tables for Hoops, Rumble, Dropshot and Snow Day.
  • Session tracker and Teams with sessions together (Instinct Pro).
  • Playing like recalibrated; other players are no longer rated or tracked.
