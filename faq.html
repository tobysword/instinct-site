FAQ

Questions and answers.

Can't find yours? Ask on Discord or use Feedback inside the app.

Safety

Can I get banned for using Instinct?

Instinct reads only the Stats API, a feature Psyonix built into Rocket League that sends live match data to programs on your own PC. It doesn't use BakkesMod, doesn't read game memory, doesn't inject code, never presses keys and never sends anything to game chat. Following the game's rules is still your responsibility; see section 5 of the terms.

Does it change my game files?

Only one setting, with your OK: the setup check sets PacketSendRate=30 in DefaultStatsAPI.ini to turn the Stats API on, and backs up the original first. That's the switch Psyonix provides for this.

Is it safe to stream with?

Yes. Turn on Hide player names in Settings and other players show as Teammate and Opponent on your overlay.

The read

What is "Playing like"?

The rank your game looks like in this playlist. In Duel, Doubles and Standard it comes from an engine that learned from about 39,000 real ranked games; other modes compare each stat with that rank's average. Your actual rank is never an input, so a great game reads high and a bad one reads low.

Why does it change so much from game to game?

One game is a small sample: a single game's read moves about 1.8 tiers on average. That's why Progress leads with your level lately (your last ten rated games). Over ten games, it averages within one rank of a player's real rank 84 to 87 % of the time. Judge a change over several games, never one.

What's my "target rank"?

The rank you think you belong at, or want to reach. Gap bars and "what decided it" compare you with that rank's average. It never changes your Playing like.

Does it coach rotation or positioning live?

No. The live Stats API doesn't send car positions, passes, possession, boost pads or whiffs, and it sends no car data for opponents. So the live overlay sticks to what it can see. If you save a replay, that match gets your positioning (free) and, with Pro, heatmaps, rotation and kickoffs.

Does it rate the other players in my lobby?

No. Other players aren't rated or tracked. Their names and that game's scoreboard line stay in your own match record on your PC.

Setup

The overlay doesn't show in game.

Check three things: Rocket League is in Borderless or Windowed mode (Windows can't draw over exclusive Fullscreen), the setup check is all green, and you restarted Rocket League after turning on the Stats API. Ctrl+Shift+O shows or hides the overlay.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can check the file against SHA256SUMS.txt first; see Download.

A hotkey doesn't work.

Another app (AMD Radeon software, Discord and others) may already use it. Settings › Hotkeys tells you when that happens, and every hotkey can be changed.

Does it work on console, Mac or Linux?

No. It needs Rocket League running on the same Windows 10 or 11 PC.

Do I need to save replays?

No. Every screen is complete without them. When you save one in Rocket League, Instinct reads it on your PC and that match gets a sharper read and your positioning.

Instinct Pro and your account

What's free and what's Pro?

Free: the live overlay, Playing like, gap bars, the post-game breakdown, saved-replay positioning and the last 30 days of history. Pro adds sessions, full history, Train next, teams, the replay deep dive, clips, share cards, the layout editor, the phone companion and cloud sync. Compare plans.

Do I need an account?

Not for the free app. You need one for Instinct Pro, teams and cloud sync. Create it in the app (Settings › Account) or on this site; it's the same account.

I forgot my password.

Go to Reset password, enter your email and open the link we send you. It brings you back to this site to choose a new password, which then works in the app too.

How do I cancel?

On your account page or in the app under Pro, choose Manage subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Cancel during the free trial and you pay nothing.

Privacy

What data do you collect?

Without an account, nothing leaves your PC except update checks to GitHub. There are no ads, no analytics and no selling of data. If you sign in with Pro and turn on cloud sync, your match summaries and sessions are stored in your account. Feedback and crash reports are sent only when you choose. Details: privacy policy.

Where are my files?

In %APPDATA%\rl-companion. Open it from Settings in the app. It's yours to copy or delete.
