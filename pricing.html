Questions

Pricing FAQ

Is it free?

The core is. The live overlay, Playing like, post-match Reads, local match history and sign-in are free. Instinct Pro ($4.99/month, or $35.88/year with 7 days free) adds the session tracker, teams, clips, the layout editor, full history, Train next drills, the replay deep dive, share cards, the phone companion, growth trends and cloud sync.

Do I need an account?

Not to play. The live overlay, post-match Reads and local match history work without one. Sign-in is free and lets you buy or redeem Instinct Pro. Tester and promo codes unlock Instinct Pro.

How does the free trial work?

Pick yearly or monthly and add a card. Nothing is charged for 7 days. Cancel in the app before the trial ends and you pay nothing; otherwise your plan starts. One trial per account.

How do refunds work?

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment, and cancel any time. See the Terms.
