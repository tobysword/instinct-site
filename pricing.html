Pricing FAQ

Is it free?

The core is. The live overlay, post-match Reads, local match history and sign-in are free. Instinct Pro ($2.99/month) adds Toxic mode, cloud sync, team sharing, cloud export, session tracking, local growth tracking and the phone companion.

Do I need an account?

Not to play. The live overlay, post-match Reads and local match history work without one. Sign-in is free and lets you buy or redeem Instinct Pro. Tester and promo codes unlock Instinct Pro.

How do refunds work?

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment, and cancel any time. See the Terms.
