Pricing FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, everything on your PC is free, including local history. Instinct Pro ($2.99/month) adds Toxic mode, cloud sync, team sharing and cloud history export.

Do I need an account?

No. Coaching works fully on your PC. An account is only needed for Instinct Pro cloud features like sync across PCs and Teams.

How do refunds work?

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment, and cancel any time. See the Terms.
