Billing

Straight answers about paying.

How does the free trial work?

Start it on this site or from the Pro page in the app. You add a card at checkout, Pro turns on straight away, and you're charged only when the 7 days end. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. Each account gets one trial.

How do I cancel or switch plans?

Go to your account on this site, or Pro in the app, and choose Manage subscription. You can cancel, switch between monthly and yearly, change your card and download invoices. If you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Who handles the payment?

Stripe. Checkout runs on Link, Stripe's payment service, which also adds and pays any sales tax or VAT and sends your receipts. We never see your card details.

Can I get a refund?

See the refund policy in our terms, or contact us.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Your matches stay on your PC; the app goes back to showing the last 30 days, and everything shows again if you come back to Pro.

I have a code from a creator or tester program.

Sign in to your account and enter it under "Have a code?". It works in the app too.
