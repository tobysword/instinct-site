What's new

Recent releases.

The app updates itself. Full notes for every version are on GitHub releases.

2.5.0

6 November 2026
  • Replay analysis, now free and much deeper. Save a replay and that match shows your rotation mistakes, what each touch led to, where your shots crossed the goal line, how cleanly you landed and recovered, which mechanics came off, and the moment behind each goal against.
  • Progress tracks these over your replayed games; Sessions lists the games you haven't saved a replay for yet.
  • Heatmaps, rotation, kickoffs and the Deep dive are no longer {{PRO_NAME}}: all replay analysis is free.
  • In game, a Session panel ({{PRO_NAME}}) shows your record, streak and this session's games.

2.4.1

5 November 2026
  • The post-game card draws its stats like the app: a bar from your target rank's average, with a short legend that no longer gets cut off.
  • Stats say Better or Worse (what higher or lower ranks do) instead of above or below.
  • A match started straight after free play now gets its post-game card and counts in your session.
  • A cleaner Match page: the target rank picker sits in the last game's card.

2.4.0

5 November 2026
  • {{PRO_NAME}} subscriptions: {{PRICE_MONTHLY}} a month or {{PRICE_YEARLY}} a year, with a {{TRIAL_DAYS}}-day free trial. Pro turns on by itself after checkout.
  • Sessions show how you played with the same stat bars as Match, biggest changes first.
  • Positioning from a saved replay is one clear visual on Match, Progress and Sessions.
  • Sessions only call a playlist above or below your usual after 3 rated games and a gap bigger than the normal game-to-game swing.

2.3.0

5 November 2026
  • A new Playing like engine for Duel, Doubles and Standard, trained on about 39,000 real ranked games. Ten games now average within one rank of a player's real rank 84 to 87 % of the time.
  • Saved replays sharpen that match: your positioning and a sharper read, read automatically on your PC.
  • A redesigned app: Match, Progress, Sessions and Team each lead with one clear answer.
  • Every stat explains itself, and the post-game card stays for the whole post-game lobby.

  • Session tracker and Teams with sessions together ({{PRO_NAME}}).
