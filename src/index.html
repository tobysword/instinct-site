Honest limits

What it can't see.

The live Stats API has no passes, possession, boost pads or boost steals, car positions or rotation, or whiffs, and no car state for opponents: opponents are events and scoreboard only.

So the live overlay never claims those. It doesn't coach rotation, positioning or boost management. Possession, passes and positions appear only in the replay Deep dive Experimental, from replays you saved.

  • Passes and possession
  • Boost pads and boost steals
  • Car positions and rotation
  • Whiffs
  • Opponents' boost and speed (no car state for them)
