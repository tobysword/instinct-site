Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16 (beta) Last updated: 2026-10-16

{{PRODUCT_NAME}} ("the app", "we", "us") is a free Windows desktop app for Rocket League coaching, made by an individual developer in the United States. Installers and updates are downloaded from GitHub Releases.

Short version: Coaching runs on your PC. Without an account, your match data stays on your computer. There are no ads, no analytics and no selling of data. Data leaves your PC only when you choose to: an optional account (cloud sync, teams, {{PRO_NAME}}), opt-in crash reports, or a feedback report you send.

1. Who is responsible for your data

  • Controller / operator: Toby Sword, an individual developer in the United States.
  • Contact: contact us via Discord or in-app Feedback.
  • EU/UK representative (GDPR Art. 27): not appointed during the free beta, because the app is not targeted at the EU/UK as a paid service. To be reviewed before paid plans launch.

2. What the app collects and where it goes

Local beta features (processed on your PC)

  • Screen analysis (off by default): reads the Rocket League window in memory to recognise the ball and cars. Frames are never saved or uploaded. A separate Record training frames switch (off by default) saves small game frames to %APPDATA%\rl-companion\training on your PC, capped at 2 GB. Frames can show on-screen names and chat. They are never uploaded; delete the folder any time. There is currently no way to contribute frames or replays to us; if we add one, it will be a separate opt-in with its own consent screen.
  • Replay analysis (off by default): reads replays you saved in your Rocket League Demos folder and keeps the newest 20 analyses locally. Nothing is uploaded.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking, no advertising, no selling or "sharing" of personal information (as defined by CCPA/CPRA).
  • No reading of game memory, no code injection, no hooks into the game process, no automated key presses.

3. Phone companion (local network)

The app can serve a companion page to your phone over your local network. All data routes need the 6-character pairing code; repeated wrong codes from one device are blocked. Traffic is plain HTTP on your LAN and does not pass through our servers, so someone already on your Wi-Fi with packet-capture tools could read it: pair on networks you trust. The pairing code stays the same until you reset it (delete token from config.json and restart).

4. Third parties

  • GitHub: update checks and downloads. GitHub sees your IP address and basic request info.
  • Supabase (database and authentication, region us-east-1): only if you create an account or send feedback.
  • Airtable: our private issue tracker. Feedback reports are copied there so we can triage them (your account email is included so we can reply).
  • Sentry: only if you opt in to crash reports and once a crash-report destination is configured. Reports are scrubbed of tokens, Windows user names in file paths, your player name and IP addresses, and include app version, OS version and stack traces. Sentry stores them in the US for up to 90 days.
  • Email delivery provider (planned, e.g. Resend): sends account emails such as confirmation and password reset.
  • Rocket League Tracker and other links: opened only when you click them; their own policies apply.
  • Psyonix / Epic Games: the app reads data the game exposes locally. We are not affiliated with them and send them nothing.

5. Account and cloud sync (optional)

Coaching works without an account. If you create one: we store your email, a hashed password (we never see your password), a display name and your plan. With cloud sync ({{PRO_NAME}} or beta tester), the app uploads match summaries, play sessions, growth snapshots and player-profile observations (platform IDs, in-game names, ranks and coaching stats of players you met) so they follow you across PCs. Teams share aggregate metrics between members you invite. Row-level security means each account can only read its own data (and team aggregates of teams it belongs to).

6. Feedback and bug reports

The in-app Send feedback form sends what you type, the kind of report, the game mode you choose, the app version and update channel, and a one-line platform summary (Windows version, screen resolution, Stats API setting, Steam/Epic). Logs are attached only if you tick the box: the event and performance logs from your last two app sessions, with your player name, Windows user name, IP addresses and tokens removed. Raw match streams, replays and screenshots are never attached, and you can preview exactly what is attached before sending. Reports and log files are deleted 30 days after they are sent. You can also save a report to a file instead and share it however you like.

7. Payments (planned {{PRO_NAME}} subscription)

Payments are not live yet. When they are, they will be handled by a merchant of record (planned: Lemon Squeezy), which processes your payment, billing address and tax information under its own privacy policy. We will receive your email, country, subscription status and a customer/subscription ID. We never see your full card number. Beta tester access is granted with a code and involves no payment.

8. Changes to Rocket League config files

At your request, the app edits Rocket League's DefaultStatsAPI.ini to enable the Stats API. It makes a backup first and changes nothing else.

9. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Legitimate interests: local processing of match data (including other players' in-game names and stats) to provide coaching; update checks; handling feedback you send.
  • Contract: your account, cloud sync, teams and {{PRO_NAME}}.
  • Consent: crash reports and attaching logs to feedback.

Data that never leaves your device is under your own control, and purely personal use may fall outside GDPR.

10. Retention

  • Local match rollups: 90 days; daily aggregates: 2 years (deleted automatically).
  • Local diagnostics: 14 days per session folder, 3 GB cap.
  • Local player profiles and self profile: until you delete them or the app's data folder.
  • Account data and cloud sync data: while your account exists; deleted within 30 days of an account-deletion request.
  • Feedback reports and attached logs: 30 days (deleted automatically).
  • Crash reports at Sentry: up to 90 days.
  • Billing records: as the merchant of record and tax law require.

11. Your rights and controls

  • Delete local data: delete the folder %APPDATA%\rl-companion (Settings → Privacy → Open data folder). Uninstalling does not remove it automatically.
  • Account: sign out any time; to delete your account and all cloud data, contact us via Discord or in-app Feedback, confirming the account's email address (an in-app delete button is planned).
  • Crash reports: turn them off any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • GDPR (EEA/UK): rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object, data portability, withdraw consent, and complain to your supervisory authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): rights to know, delete and correct, and to not be discriminated against. We do not sell or share personal information.

To exercise your rights, contact us via Discord or in-app Feedback. We reply within 30 days. We may need to verify that you own the account.

Other players: profiles of people you played with live on your PC, and in your cloud account only if you use cloud sync. If you believe your data is in our cloud database, contact us via Discord or in-app Feedback and we will help.

12. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (16 in the EEA/UK, or the applicable age in your country). Accounts and purchases require the minimum ages in the Terms. If you believe a child created an account or sent us data, contact us via Discord or in-app Feedback and we will delete it.

13. Security

Local data is stored unencrypted in your Windows user profile and protected by your PC's account security. Cloud data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Supabase, and protected by row-level security. No system is perfectly secure; keep your PC and network protected.

14. International transfers

Account, feedback and crash data are processed in the United States (Supabase us-east-1, Airtable, Sentry). Transfers from the EEA/UK rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses.

15. Changes

We post updates here and in the repository, update the date above, and tell you in the app about material changes.
