Privacy and data

Privacy Policy

The short version

  • Game data stays on your PC. The Stats API is local-only (127.0.0.1). Saved replays are read on your PC and never uploaded. Phone traffic stays on your Wi-Fi.
  • No ads, no analytics, no data selling.
  • Leaves your PC only if you choose: an account and cloud sync ({{PRO_NAME}}: your own match summaries, play sessions and target rank), opt-in crash reports (off by default; asked once; scrubbed of names, paths and IPs) and feedback you send. Update checks go to GitHub.
  • Other players are not tracked (since 2.2). Their in-match names and that game's scoreboard line stay in your match records on your PC; nothing about them goes to the cloud. Older lobby data was deleted from the cloud on 4 November 2026.
  • This website has no analytics, ads or cookies. If you sign in here, your sign-in is kept in this browser's local storage until you sign out. Payments are handled by Stripe, with Link as the merchant of record.
  • Your files: %APPDATA%\rl-companion (settings, match history, sessions, diagnostics). Open it from Settings → Privacy → Open data folder; delete it any time.
  • Cloud data is kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete it. Deletion removes it from the live database right away; Supabase backups may hold it until they expire under Supabase's backup retention.
  • Deleting your data: to delete your account or cloud data, contact us.

Features processed only on your PC

  • Saved replays (always on since 2.3): Rocket League only saves a replay when you choose to. The app reads the replays in your Rocket League Demos folder, on this PC only, and adds that match's positioning and a sharper Playing like to its record. It keeps the newest 20 analyses and a list of replay file names it has read. Nothing is uploaded, and replay files are never sent anywhere. Replays contain every player's in-game name; the app keeps only what is described in the match history row above.
  • Clips ({{PRO_NAME}}, off by default): clips stay local, are never uploaded, and the buffer is RAM only. While Clips is on, the last 15 to 60 seconds of the Rocket League window (video only, no sound or microphone) are kept in memory and written to disk only when you press the save hotkey or button, as an MP4 in your Videos\Instinct folder named by date and time. Clips can show on-screen names, chat and in-game overlays (such as Discord or Steam). Turning Clips off frees the memory; delete clips from the Clips page or the folder any time.
  • Screen analysis (not offered since 1.0: no setting, and a 0.13 opt-in is switched off on update). Older versions could read the Rocket League window in memory and, with a separate switch, save small training frames to %APPDATA%\rl-companion\training. Nothing was ever uploaded; you can delete that folder if it exists.

Toxic mode (hidden since 2.2)

Toxic mode is not offered in the app at the moment (there is no setting for it). If it returns it will be {{PRO_NAME}} and off by default, and work as before: lines are picked and shown on your PC only, never sent to game chat, never read aloud, and never included in logs, diagnostics, feedback reports, match summaries or cloud sync; only anonymous line ids are kept locally (last 400 or 14 days) so lines don't repeat. Lines never contain anyone's name. Toxic settings (including the 18+ confirmation) are stored only in your local settings.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking (in the app or on this website), no advertising, no tracking cookies, no selling or "sharing" of personal information (as defined by CCPA/CPRA).
  • No tracking or rating of other players, and nothing about them in the cloud.
  • No reading of game memory, no code injection, no hooks into the game process, no automated key presses, and no BakkesMod.
  • The app never uploads replays and never contacts replay sites such as ballchasing.com.

3. Phone companion (local network)

The app can serve a companion page to your phone over your local network ({{PRO_NAME}}). All data routes need the 6-character pairing code; repeated wrong codes from one device are blocked. Traffic is plain HTTP on your LAN and does not pass through our servers, so someone already on your Wi-Fi with packet-capture tools could read it: pair on networks you trust. The pairing code stays the same until you reset it (delete token from config.json and restart). The phone never holds your account sign-in: team and account requests from the phone are passed through the app on your PC, with email addresses removed from the replies.

4. Third parties

  • GitHub: update checks and downloads, and hosting of this website (GitHub Pages). GitHub sees your IP address and basic request info.
  • Supabase (database and authentication, region us-east-1): only if you create an account, sign in on this website or send feedback.
  • Stripe (Stripe Managed Payments, with Link as merchant of record): only if you start an {{PRO_NAME}} checkout or manage your subscription (see §7). Checkout and the billing portal open in your web browser on Stripe's and Link's pages.
  • Airtable: our private issue tracker. Feedback reports are copied there so we can triage them (your account email is included so we can reply).
  • Sentry: crash reporting is opt-in and off by default: the first-run consent screen asks once, and you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy. Nothing is sent unless you said yes (and the build has a destination configured). Before sending, every report and breadcrumb is scrubbed of tokens and codes, the Windows user name in file paths, IP addresses, platform ids (Steam, Epic, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch) and player names: the name set in Settings and any value stored in a player-name field are replaced before anything leaves your PC (covered by automated tests; default PII sending is off). A name typed in free text that the app cannot recognise as a name may remain. Reports include the app version, OS version and stack traces. Sentry stores them in the US for up to 90 days.
  • Email: account emails (confirmation, password reset) are sent by Supabase today; a dedicated email delivery provider (e.g. Resend) is planned.
  • Rocket League Tracker and other links: opened only when you click them; their own policies apply.
  • Psyonix / Epic Games: the app reads data the game exposes locally and replay files you saved. We are not affiliated with them and send them nothing.

5. Account and cloud sync (optional)

Coaching works without an account. If you create one (in the app or on this website's Account page): we store your email, a hashed password (we never see your password), a display name and your plan.

Website Account page. You can sign in on this website to see your plan, start a free trial or subscription, manage it, reset your password or redeem a code. Your sign-in token, account ID and email are kept in that browser's local storage until you sign out; the page talks directly to Supabase (and, for billing, to Stripe through our server). This website sets no cookies and has no analytics.

Cloud sync (automatic with {{PRO_NAME}} or a tester code while you are signed in in the app) uploads, for you only:

  • one match summary per match: playlist, time, result, score, mode, your own goals, shots, saves, assists, demos and touches, your Playing like and the ids of that game's coaching reads (never their text);
  • one row per play session: start and end, number of games, wins and losses by mode, free play and training pack time;
  • your target rank per playlist, with your own platform ID (for example your Steam or Epic ID);
  • weekly growth snapshots built from your synced matches.

Cloud sync never sends other players' names, IDs, stats or ranks, teammates' names, the raw stat feed, replays, clips or screen frames. Since 2.2 the server also refuses any other player's data, and the lobby observations earlier versions uploaded (other players' platform IDs, names, rank estimates and rank sightings) were deleted from the cloud on 4 November 2026.

Teams ({{PRO_NAME}}): members of a team you create or join can see your display name, per-playlist totals and recent results from your synced matches, and which sessions you played together (matched by match id). Row-level security means each account can only read its own data and the team views of teams it belongs to.

6. Feedback and bug reports

The in-app Send feedback form sends what you type, the kind of report, the game mode you choose, the app version and update channel, and a one-line platform summary (Windows version, screen resolution, Stats API setting, Steam/Epic). Logs are attached only if you tick the box: the event and performance logs from your last two app sessions, with your player name, Windows user name, IP addresses and tokens removed. Raw match streams, replays and screenshots are never attached, and you can preview exactly what is attached before sending. Reports and log files are deleted 30 days after they are sent: the report itself and attached logs are deleted from Supabase, and the copy in our tracker has its text, your email and the log link removed (only the report type, status and app version are kept for triage history). You can also save a report to a file instead and share it however you like.

7. Payments ({{PRO_NAME}} subscription)

{{PRO_NAME}} costs US$4.99/month or US$35.88/year (plus applicable taxes), with one 7-day free trial per account; the price is shown at checkout before you pay. Payments are handled by Stripe Managed Payments, with Link (Stripe's checkout) as the merchant of record. Link processes your payment details, billing address and tax information under its own privacy policy, and handles receipts, tax and billing support. When you start checkout we send Stripe your account email and an internal account ID so the subscription is tied to your {{PRODUCT_NAME}} account. We receive and keep your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, the plan and price, the subscription status, the renewal or trial end date, whether you cancelled, and whether you have used your free trial. We never see or store your card number. Beta tester access is granted with a code and involves no payment. When you redeem a code we record which code (as a one-way hash) your account used and when, and we log redeem attempts (account ID and time) to stop code guessing.

8. Changes to Rocket League config files

At your request, the app edits Rocket League's DefaultStatsAPI.ini to enable the Stats API. It makes a backup first and changes nothing else. The app only reads your Demos folder; it does not change or delete your replays.

10. Retention

  • Local match history (instinct.db): kept until you delete it (Settings › Privacy › Open data folder). Nothing is trimmed automatically. Free accounts see the last 30 days; older matches stay on your PC.
  • Local session rollups for the dashboard: 90 days; daily totals: 2 years (deleted automatically).
  • Local replay analyses: the newest 20.
  • Local diagnostics: 14 days per session folder, 3 GB cap.
  • Local self profile and sessions: until you delete them or the app's data folder.
  • Cloud data (account data, synced match summaries, play sessions, growth snapshots, your target rank, team membership): kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete it. There is no automatic expiry. An account-deletion request is completed within 30 days.
  • Deleting data removes it from our live database right away. Backups follow Supabase's backup retention: they may still hold deleted data until they expire.
  • Website sign-in: in your browser until you sign out (or clear site data).
  • Feedback reports and attached logs: 30 days (report rows and log files deleted automatically; the tracker copy is anonymised at the same time).
  • Beta tester redemption records: while your account exists (needed to keep or end your tester access); deleted with your account.
  • Redeem-attempt logs: used only for rate limiting; deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Subscription records (Stripe IDs, plan, status, dates, trial used): while your account exists. Whether you have used your free trial may be kept after deletion to stop repeat trials.
  • Crash reports at Sentry: up to 90 days.
  • Billing records held by Stripe / Link: as the merchant of record and tax law require.

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (16 in the EEA/UK, or the applicable age in your country). Accounts and purchases require the minimum ages in the Terms. If you believe a child created an account or sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12a. Health data (Washington My Health My Data Act)

The app does not collect, process or share consumer health data as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373). Gameplay statistics, coaching reads and Playing like readings are not health data, so that Act does not apply. If this ever changes, we will publish a separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy first.

13. Security

Local data is stored unencrypted in your Windows user profile and protected by your PC's account security. Cloud data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Supabase, and protected by row-level security. The app and this website only ever hold the public (anonymous) database key; your plan can only be changed by our server and Stripe's signed webhooks. No system is perfectly secure; keep your PC and network protected.

14. International transfers

Account, feedback, billing and crash data are processed in the United States (Supabase us-east-1, Stripe / Link, Airtable, Sentry). Transfers from the EEA/UK rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses.
