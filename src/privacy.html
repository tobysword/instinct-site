Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Instinct ("the app", "we", "us") is a Windows app for Rocket League coaching, made and operated by Toby Sword, an individual developer in Washington State, United States. This policy covers the app, its online services (accounts, cloud sync, teams and feedback) and this website.

In short

Coaching runs on your PC. We keep your account details and, with {{PRO_NAME}}, a summary of your own matches. There are no ads, no analytics and no selling of data, and we don't track other players.

1. What stays on your PC

  • Live match data from Rocket League's local Stats API, including other players' in-game names and stats during the match.
  • Your match history, sessions, profile and settings, in %APPDATA%\rl-companion. Each match record keeps that game's scoreboard, including the other players' in-game names.
  • Replays you save in Rocket League. The app reads them on your PC and never uploads them.
  • Clips ({{PRO_NAME}}, off by default): clips stay local, are never uploaded, and the buffer is RAM only. A clip is video of the Rocket League window with no sound, saved to Videos\Instinct only when you ask. It can show anything on screen, such as names, chat or other overlays.
  • Diagnostics logs, deleted after 14 days.

The app does not read game memory, inject code or press keys, and it does not open a network port.

3. Who handles data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and account emails.
  • Stripe and Link: payments. Link is the merchant of record and handles your payment details, tax and receipts under its own privacy policy. We send Stripe your email and account ID so the subscription matches your account. We never see your card.
  • Airtable: our feedback tracker.
  • Sentry: crash reports, only if you turn them on.
  • GitHub: downloads, updates and website hosting.

These services process data in the United States under their own terms.

4. What we don't do

No ads, no analytics in the app or on the website, no cookies on the website, and no selling or sharing of personal information. If you sign in on the website, your sign-in is kept in your browser's local storage until you sign out.

5. How long we keep data

  • Data on your PC: until you delete it.
  • Account and cloud data: until you delete your account or ask us to. Backups may hold deleted data until they expire.
  • Match records shared with a team: 60 days.
  • Feedback and attached logs: 30 days.
  • Redeem attempts: 30 days.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Billing records at Stripe and Link: as long as tax law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete data on your PC: delete the %APPDATA%\rl-companion folder (Settings › Account & data › Open data folder).
  • Delete a match: from its breakdown on Progress. This also removes it from the cloud.
  • Stop cloud sync: sign out.
  • Crash reports: turn them on or off in Settings › Account & data.
  • Delete your account and cloud data: ask us on Discord or through in-app Feedback. We do it within 30 days. Cancel your subscription first, or ask us to cancel it.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to use these rights; we reply within 30 days.

For users in the EEA and UK, we rely on contract (your account, cloud sync, teams and {{PRO_NAME}}), legitimate interests (handling feedback, security and preventing abuse) and consent (crash reports and attaching logs to feedback).

7. Children

Instinct is not for children under 13 (16 in the EEA and UK). If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data sent to our services is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each account can only read its own data and its teams' data. Data on your PC is protected by your Windows account.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. We tell you in the app about important changes.
