Toxic mode Instinct Pro

Roasts you, not chat.

Optional mature humour on your overlay only. Never sent to the game.

THE FACTS

Off until you turn it on.

  • Part of Instinct Pro. Off by default.
  • One line in some goal breaks, under the Reads.
  • Savagery 1-3.
  • Swearing is a separate toggle, off by default.
  • Swearing, Savagery 3 and the phone mirror need an 18+ confirmation.
  • Lines never contain anyone's name: only "you", "your teammate", "the lobby" or "them".
  • Teammate lines are opt-in and mild, and never in 1v1.
  • It never roasts a play that just worked.
Settings

You choose how far it goes.

Toxic mode and Swearing both start switched off. The strongest options ask you to confirm you're 18 or older.

Safety

Checked twice. Kept private.

Blocklist, twice

Every line is checked against a slur and abuse blocklist when it's built, and again on screen.

Signed off by a person

The harshest lines are signed off by a person, and disabled if their text changes.

Nothing logged

Nothing Toxic is logged or synced. Never sent to game chat, never read aloud.

Streamers: if you stream, you're responsible for what your overlay shows. Instinct warns you once. See also Hide player names and the Toxic mode terms.

Part of Instinct Pro.

Instinct Pro is $2.99/month. Everything else in Instinct is free.
